06 / How to explain this in your demonstration

1. Ready queue

Processes enter the FIFO ready queue when they arrive. The CPU selects the process at the front.

2. Time slice

The selected process runs for the smaller of the time quantum or its remaining burst time. If unfinished, it returns to the queue.

3. Evaluate

The simulation records execution intervals and derives CT, TAT, WT, RT, averages and context switches for each quantum.

Important implementation rule: processes arriving during a time slice are added to the ready queue before the preempted process is re-enqueued at the end of its slice.
